  => IT COLLAPSES THE COLLECTIVE (a deep negative): selecting the credited PARTS inside
     the deme does not compound — competence 0.363 < deme-only credit 0.615, and
     network survival falls to 0.29 of demes (vs 0.43). Biasing reproduction
     toward high-credit parts erodes the DIVERSITY the cross-production network needs, so
     the very collective that made those parts valuable falls apart. Competence is an
     IRREDUCIBLY COLLECTIVE property — not a sum of part contributions you can select
     independently — so part-level selection on credit is self-defeating. The arc's
     terminal diagnosis: compounding needs a part-level REPLICATOR (a part that carries
     and copies its own value out of context — the exp012 von Neumann lesson), which the
     type-path parts fundamentally lack.
